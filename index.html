A few things to know.

Is Hidy Vault just a hidden photo album?

Hidy stores imported files in its own encrypted vault. It does not simply hide items in the Photos app. Originals remain in Photos unless you choose to delete them, and deleted Photos items can remain in Recently Deleted.

What happens if I forget my pattern?

A saved recovery phrase can restore access to that vault. Without the pattern or a valid recovery phrase, we cannot reset access or decrypt the vault for you.

Can I restore on another iPhone?

Use an encrypted backup file or an iCloud backup together with the vault's recovery phrase. Saving the phrase is essential; knowing the pattern alone is not a replacement for that backup recovery process.

Do I need an account?

No Hidy account is required to use a local vault. Optional iCloud features use your Apple account. Apple or Polar processes purchases and license checks.

Can I take back a shared file?

You can revoke future vault sharing access, but the recipient retains downloaded or exported copies. Share only with people you trust.

What devices does Hidy Vault support?

Hidy Vault is built for iPhone running iOS 16 or later. Public App Store release is coming. There is no public iPhone download link yet.
