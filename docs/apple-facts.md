# Apple facts already verified (support.apple.com, fetched 2026-10-22)

Use only these for iOS claims unless the loop's Perplexity research step returns more with a
support.apple.com citation. Cite the URL in "sources".

- Hidden album (https://support.apple.com/en-us/104987): hiding moves photos out of Library,
  other albums and the Photos widget. iOS 16+ the Hidden album is locked by default. Hide:
  touch and hold the photo or video > Hide, then confirm. Unhide: in the Hidden album, touch and
  hold > Unhide. NEVER write "share button" or "share sheet" for Hide/Unhide — not in these facts. View: Collections > Utilities > Hidden > View Album > Face ID/Touch ID.
  Settings > Apps > Photos > Show Hidden Album off removes the album. With iCloud Photos, hidden
  on one device = hidden on others.
- Delete/hide (https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/delete-or-hide-photos-and-videos-iphb4defbde9/ios):
  Hidden and Recently Deleted unlock with Face ID, Touch ID or passcode; Settings > Apps > Photos
  > Use Face ID toggles the lock. Recently Deleted keeps items 30 days. Select > More > Hide for
  several.
- Lock or hide an app (https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/lock-or-hide-an-app-iph00f208d05/ios):
  iOS 18+. Touch and hold > Require Face ID; Hide and Require Face ID moves it to a Hidden folder
  in App Library. Built-in apps can be locked, not hidden. Locked app content stays out of
  search, notification previews, Siri suggestions. Relocks when quit. Status doesn't sync via
  iCloud. Under-13s in Family Sharing can't lock/hide.
- Lock notes (https://support.apple.com/en-us/102537): iOS 16+ lock with iPhone passcode or a
  separate notes password; end-to-end encrypted for iCloud accounts. Can't lock notes with tags,
  PDFs, audio, video, Keynote/Pages/Numbers, IMAP notes or shared notes. Locked notes stay open
  several minutes; forgotten notes password = no access.
- Guided Access (https://support.apple.com/en-us/111795): locks iPhone to one app.
- Location metadata (https://support.apple.com/guide/personal-safety/manage-location-metadata-in-photos-ips0d7a5df82/web):
  More > Adjust Info > Adjust Location > No Location; share sheet Options > Location off;
  Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Camera > Never.
- Before selling (https://support.apple.com/en-us/109511): back up, sign out, Settings > General >
  Transfer or Reset iPhone > Erase All Content and Settings. Erasing doesn't delete iCloud data.
- Repair (https://support.apple.com/en-us/109519, https://support.apple.com/en-us/122189): back
  up first; device may come back erased; newer iOS has Ready for Repair in Find My.
- iCloud data security overview: https://support.apple.com/en-us/102651
- Personal Safety User Guide: https://support.apple.com/guide/personal-safety/welcome/web

## Added 2026-10-23 (fetched from support.apple.com)
- iCloud Photos (https://support.apple.com/en-us/108782): keeps every photo and video in iCloud,
  originals at full resolution; changes on one device change on the others. Turn on: Settings >
  [your name] > iCloud > Photos > Sync this device. Deleting a photo or video on one device deletes
  it everywhere iCloud Photos is used; recover from Recently Deleted within 30 days. Optimize
  Storage keeps smaller versions on the device.
- Delete an app (https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/remove-or-delete-apps-iph248b543ca/ios):
  Remove from Home Screen moves the app to App Library (not deleted). Delete App removes the app
  AND its data from the iPhone. Data the app stored in iCloud is not deleted with the app.
  Previously downloaded apps can be reinstalled from App Store > your picture > Apps.
- iCloud Backup (https://support.apple.com/en-us/108770): backs up data that isn't already synced
  to iCloud, including app data for downloaded apps, device settings and Home Screen layout. With
  iCloud Photos on, photos/videos sync to iCloud and are not in the daily backup; without it, the
  backup may include photos and videos.
- Data protection (https://support.apple.com/en-us/102651): standard data protection is the
  default — iCloud data is encrypted, keys are held in Apple data centers so Apple can help with
  recovery, and only certain data is end-to-end encrypted. Advanced Data Protection (optional)
  end-to-end encrypts most iCloud data including iCloud Backup, Photos and Notes; only trusted
  devices hold the keys, not even Apple can access it.
- Advanced Data Protection (https://support.apple.com/en-us/108756): Settings > [your name] >
  iCloud > Advanced Data Protection > Turn on. Applies to the whole account. iCloud.com web access
  is disabled by default. Shared Albums in Photos and "anyone with the link" sharing are NOT
  end-to-end encrypted even with ADP; iCloud Shared Photo Library is, if all participants use ADP.
- Stolen Device Protection (https://support.apple.com/en-us/120340): iOS 17.3+, must be on before
  loss. Away from familiar locations some actions (stored passwords, credit cards) need Face ID or
  Touch ID with no passcode fallback; some (changing Apple Account password) need an hour's
  security delay. It protects account/device changes; it does not mention the Photos app.
- iCloud Shared Photo Library (https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/use-icloud-shared-photo-library-iph28ac9ea81/ios):
  up to 5 other people; one shared library at a time; photos you contribute move from your
  personal library and all members can view, edit and delete them. Needs iCloud Photos, iOS 16.1+.
- App access to Photos (https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/control-access-to-information-in-apps-iph251e92810/ios):
  apps ask the first time; change later in Settings > Privacy & Security > [category]; App
  Privacy Report shows how apps use permissions.
