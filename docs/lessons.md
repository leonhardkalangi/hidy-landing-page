# Lessons from past fact-checks

Maintained by scripts/seo-loop.mjs from claims the reviewer removed; fed into the writer's prompt.
Prohibitions only — facts live in hidy-facts.md / apple-facts.md. Safe to edit by hand.

- Don't name a button, menu item or UI path for importing from Photos, choosing import quality, confirming an import, or turning off Hidy's delete offer; only use the flows named in the allowed facts.
- Don't claim what happens to existing items when import quality is changed, or that a setting only affects new imports.
- Don't claim a workflow that removes location in Photos and then imports as Original, or that location can be stripped while the file is kept whole.
- Don't claim pixel dimensions, megapixel counts or file sizes of iPhone photos or of Hidy's optimized files.
- Don't name a Files app information panel or any UI path for inspecting a file's details.
- Don't claim what file Hidy receives from Photos when Optimize Storage is on, or that it may be a smaller device version.
- Don't claim Hidy's encryption does or does not re-encode, recompress or alter the file.
- Don't claim whether the Hidden album or hidden photos are encrypted, or how they are stored on the device.
- Don't claim that hidden photos can be found, recovered or accessed with tools or by someone with device access.
- Don't claim that unhiding and deleting removes photos from the Hidden album or any other iOS behaviour beyond the allowed facts.
- Don't claim that the iPhone passcode alone is enough to open the Hidden album or Recently Deleted.
- Don't claim that the Photos app cannot be deleted, or what happens to the Photos app when deleted.
- Don't claim that Hidy can be installed or downloaded, or give any onboarding step such as installing the app; say only that it is coming to the App Store.
- Don't claim what the iPhone Keychain or iCloud Keychain stores, syncs, encrypts or how it is protected, or name any Passwords or Keychain setting, button or path.
- Don't claim how the Passwords app unlocks, what it requires to open, who can open it, or that it is the front door to iCloud Keychain.
- Don't claim Apple's password or passkey sharing behaviour, shared password groups, or that sharing goes through iCloud Keychain.
- Don't claim what happens to stored passwords or passkeys when iCloud Keychain is turned off, or that they stay on the iPhone.
- Don't claim what Advanced Data Protection requires before turning it on, asks for during setup, or whether it is available by country or account.
- Don't claim what Apple can or cannot access, read or be compelled to hand over under any iCloud setting, or that Apple cannot read data.
- Don't claim what happens if account access or recovery keys are lost with Advanced Data Protection on, or name any way back in.
- Don't claim what Advanced Data Protection is, does or does not change for iCloud.com web access, or that it can be turned back on.
- Don't claim what Advanced Data Protection costs or that it is free.
- Don't claim a number of end-to-end encrypted categories, data types or features under any iCloud setting.
- Don't claim how the iOS clipboard behaves, including whether it keeps history, only one item, or clears itself, or what any paste setting or prompt does.
- Don't claim Hidy copies text out of a vault, puts vault content on the clipboard, or any Hidy clipboard behaviour.
