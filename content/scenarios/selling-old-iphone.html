The right order

  1. Back up the old iPhone and each Hidy vault.
  2. Set up the new iPhone, install Hidy Vault, and restore each vault with its recovery phrase. See transfer a vault to a new iPhone.
  3. Open every vault on the new phone and check a few files.
  4. Then erase the old one: sign out of your Apple Account and use Erase All Content and Settings, as Apple describes.

What erasing removes, and what it doesn't

  • Removes: everything on the device, including Hidy vaults and its Keychain secret.
  • Doesn't remove: your iCloud content, including encrypted Hidy backups in your iCloud account.

Checklist

  • Every vault restored and opened on the new iPhone.
  • Recovery phrases still safe (you'll need them again on the next phone).
  • Old iPhone signed out and erased.
  • Private photos you no longer want in iCloud deleted there too.
