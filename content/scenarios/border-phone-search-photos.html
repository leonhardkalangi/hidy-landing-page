What an inspection can reach

An unlocked iPhone shows everything its apps show: Photos, Messages, Files, notes, and any vault you open. What's encrypted and closed stays unreadable without its key, but you may be asked for that key too.

Travel with less

  1. Back up first. Make an encrypted Hidy backup of each vault, and keep the recovery phrases at home or with someone you trust.
  2. Remove what you don't need on the trip. A vault you can restore after you arrive doesn't need to cross the border on the phone.
  3. Keep the rest in a vault with a pattern you don't use anywhere else, not in Photos.
  4. Restore after arrival from iCloud or a backup file, using the recovery phrase.

Limits

  • Encryption doesn't prevent being asked to unlock.
  • A decoy vault deletes data, which may be unlawful during an official search.
  • Restoring needs access to your iCloud account or backup file, plus the phrase.

For your specific route, check official guidance or ask a lawyer. Organisations such as the EFF publish travel guides for digital devices.
