Why it lands so hard

A camera roll is a timeline of your life with no filter: screenshots of conversations, medical photos, an ex, a bank letter, a photo you took for yourself. One extra swipe moves from "here's my dog" to something you didn't choose to share. The embarrassment isn't about the other person. It's that you weren't in control of what they saw.

The real problem is where the photos live

Everything you take lands in the same place, and handing over the phone hands over that place. iOS doesn't have a "show only this photo" mode. Your options are to change what's in the library, or to change what you hand over.

Make your camera roll safe to hand over

  1. Sort in 10 minutes. Scroll back a few months and select anything you wouldn't show a colleague.
  2. Light stuff: Hide it. Select, then Hide. It leaves the Library, other albums and the Photos widget. Check that Settings > Apps > Photos > Use Face ID is on.
  3. Serious stuff: move it into a vault. Import into Hidy Vault, let Hidy delete the originals, then empty Recently Deleted.
  4. Going forward: take sensitive photos with Hidy's camera so they never reach your library.

Setup checklist

  • Hidden album locked with Face ID, and Show Hidden Album turned off.
  • Recently Deleted emptied after moving photos.
  • One vault for "would hate for anyone to see", with a pattern you never draw in front of people.
  • A recovery phrase for that vault, written on paper.
