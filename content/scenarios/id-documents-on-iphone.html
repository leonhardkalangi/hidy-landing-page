Why it matters

An ID scan gives someone your full name, date of birth, document number and photo in one image. That's enough to open accounts or pass identity checks in many places. Unlike a password, you can't change your passport number after a leak.

Where they hide on a typical iPhone

  • Photos: pictures taken for a rental, a bank or a visa form.
  • Files and Downloads: PDFs from email or scanning apps.
  • Messages and mail: attachments you sent to yourself.
  • iCloud: all of the above, synced to your other devices.

Move them into a vault

  1. In Hidy Vault, create a vault for documents, with its own pattern.
  2. Import the photos from Photos and the PDFs from Files.
  3. Delete the originals: Hidy offers to remove photos from your library; delete PDFs in Files yourself. Then empty Recently Deleted in both.
  4. Create the vault's recovery phrase and turn on encrypted iCloud backup. Losing these documents would be painful too.

Checklist

  • Photos search for "passport", "ID", "licence" returns nothing.
  • Files > Recently Deleted emptied.
  • Old email attachments with ID scans deleted.
  • Documents vault backed up, recovery phrase on paper.
