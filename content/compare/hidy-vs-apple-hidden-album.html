Where the Hidden album is the right choice

If you just want photos out of your main grid, out of Memories and out of the Photos widget, Hidden does that with two taps and costs nothing. It's locked by default since iOS 16, and you can turn off Show Hidden Album in Settings so the album isn't even listed.

Where it falls short

  • Same key as the phone. If Face ID fails, your passcode opens it. A partner, a child or anyone who has seen you type the passcode can get in.
  • Same library everywhere. With iCloud Photos, hidden photos are part of the library on every device signed in to your Apple Account, such as a shared iPad or an old Mac.
  • Photos only. ID scans as PDFs, notes and other files have no Hidden album.

What Hidy Vault does differently

  • A separate key. Each vault is encrypted with a key derived from its own pattern. Your iPhone passcode doesn't open it. See pattern encryption.
  • Outside the library. Imported copies live in Hidy's encrypted storage. Delete the originals and they're gone from Photos too, after Recently Deleted empties.
  • More than photos. Documents, notes and folders, plus a camera that never saves to Photos.

Where Hidy is weaker

  • No reset. Forget the pattern without a recovery phrase and the vault is gone. Apple can help you with your Apple Account; nobody can help with a lost Hidy pattern.
  • An extra app. The Hidden album is already there. Hidy has to be installed, set up and remembered.
  • No independent audit yet. Our security page lists the limits.

Moving photos from Hidden into Hidy

  1. Install Hidy Vault and create a vault.
  2. Import from Photos and select the photos. Hidy encrypts copies.
  3. Let Hidy delete the originals. iOS asks you to confirm and keeps them in Recently Deleted for up to 30 days; empty it if you want them gone now.
