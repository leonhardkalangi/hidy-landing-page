Where Apple Notes is the right choice

For passwords you can't put in a password manager, account numbers, door codes and short private records, a locked note is quick and well protected. Apple says both lock options use end-to-end encryption for iCloud accounts. If you choose a separate notes password, your iPhone passcode doesn't open those notes.

Where it falls short

  • Files can't be locked. Apple says notes with PDFs, audio, video, tags or Keynote, Pages or Numbers documents can't be locked. Scanned IDs saved as PDFs are exactly the files people want to protect.
  • Passcode mode shares the phone's key. If you lock notes with the device passcode, anyone with the passcode can open them.
  • Open for several minutes. Convenient, but a note you just viewed is still open if you hand over the phone.

What Hidy adds

  • Any file type in the same encrypted vault as photos and notes. See documents and notes.
  • A separate pattern per vault and immediate locking when you leave the app.
  • A camera that captures documents straight into the vault.

Where Hidy is weaker

  • Notes in Hidy are plain text, without Apple Notes' formatting, checklists or drawing.
  • It's another app to install and remember, and its pattern can't be reset.
