Save clean copy

What photos hide

  • Location: GPS coordinates, often accurate to a few metres, and sometimes altitude.
  • Time: when the photo was taken and last edited.
  • Device: camera make and model, lens, and the software that saved it.
  • Extra blocks: XMP and IPTC data from editing apps.

How it works

  1. Your browser reads the file. For JPEGs, the page parses the EXIF block and shows what it finds.
  2. The image is drawn onto a canvas, which keeps only the pixels.
  3. The canvas is saved as a new file. No metadata is copied across.

On iPhone, without this tool

In the share sheet, tap Options and turn off Location before you send. Or open the photo, tap More, Adjust Info, Adjust Location, No Location. See how to remove location from iPhone photos.
