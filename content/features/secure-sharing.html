Why share a vault instead of photos

Sending photos through Messages or a shared album puts readable copies in more places: the chat history, the other person's library, their backups. A shared vault keeps the files encrypted in the same place for both of you. Both see and add to the same files, and nothing lands in either camera roll.

How it works

  1. You create an invite. Hidy shows 12 words that can open this vault's key. They expire after 48 hours.
  2. You tell them the words privately, in person or in a message you trust.
  3. They join. In Hidy Vault, they choose Join a shared vault on the lock screen, type the words, and pick their own pattern.
  4. Both sync through the owner's iCloud. Files are encrypted on each device. iCloud and Hidy can't read them.

What removing someone does, and doesn't do

  • Does: stops their future syncing after the cloud service processes the change.
  • Doesn't: erase what's already on their device, or any file they exported. Nobody can promise that, so share with people you trust.

Share a vault

  1. Make a separate vault with only what you want to share. Don't share your main vault.
  2. In that vault's Settings, create an invite. Hidy needs the vault's recovery phrase set up first.
  3. Tell the person the 12 words. Make a new invite for each person.
