The problem with importing later

Every photo you take with the iPhone Camera app lands in your library first. From there it can appear in Memories, widgets, search, shared albums and iCloud Photos, before you've had a chance to move it. Even after you import it into a vault and delete it, iOS keeps the original in Recently Deleted for up to 30 days.

Hidy's camera skips all of that. The photo exists only inside the vault.

How it works

  1. Open the vault you want the photo in.
  2. Tap add, choose Take photo or video, and shoot.
  3. Hidy encrypts it into the vault. It doesn't save anything to Photos.

Good uses

  • Documents: passport, ID card, insurance card, bank letters.
  • Receipts and evidence you need to keep, but not in your everyday library.
  • Personal photos you'd never want in a Memories slideshow.

Limits

  • Camera access is required. iOS asks for permission the first time.
  • Sharing makes a readable copy. If you send a camera photo to someone, they receive a normal file.
  • The free plan limits apply: 100 photos and 10 videos per vault.
