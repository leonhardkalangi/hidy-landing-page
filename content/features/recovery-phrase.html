Why a vault needs a phrase

The pattern is part of the key, and Hidy keeps no copy of it. That's what makes the vault private. It also means that if you forget the pattern and have no phrase, nobody can open the vault, including us. The recovery phrase closes that gap without adding an account or a server.

What the phrase can do

  • Reopen a vault whose pattern you forgot, and let you choose a new pattern.
  • Open that vault's encrypted backups, from iCloud or from a backup file, on any iPhone.
  • Join nothing else. A phrase belongs to one vault. It doesn't unlock other vaults or your Apple Account.

Create it

  1. Open the vault with its pattern.
  2. Go to Settings and create the recovery phrase.
  3. Write the 12 words down in order. Hidy won't show them again.
  4. Store the paper somewhere safe and separate from the phone.

The words come from a fixed list of 2,048 words, so they're easy to write and type back.

Use it

  1. On Hidy's lock screen, open the options and choose the recovery-phrase option.
  2. Enter the 12 words. Hidy counts them as you type.
  3. Choose a new pattern for the vault.

Moving to a new iPhone? The phrase is what restores a backup there. See back up and recover a vault.

Keep it safe

  • Treat it like a key. Anyone with the phrase and a backup of that vault can open the backup.
  • Not in the same place as the phone. A screenshot or a note on the same iPhone defeats the purpose.
  • Rotate if exposed. Create a new phrase, make a fresh backup, and delete backups made with the old phrase.
