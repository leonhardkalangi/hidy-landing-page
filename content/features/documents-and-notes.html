Why documents belong in a vault

The Files app and email attachments are where ID scans, contracts, medical letters and tax papers end up. Anyone who can unlock your phone can open them. These are often more sensitive than photos, because they can be used to impersonate you.

What you can keep

  • Documents: any file you can select in Files, including PDFs, scans and spreadsheets.
  • Notes: plain-text notes written and edited inside the vault.
  • Folders: group files and move them between folders. Removing a folder keeps its files in the main vault.

How to add them

  1. Open a vault and tap add.
  2. Choose Files to import documents, or create a note or a folder.
  3. To capture a paper document without it ever reaching Photos, use Hidy's camera.

Viewing and sharing

Hidy previews documents inside the vault. Viewing decrypts a temporary copy on your device. Hidy clears those temporary files when you close the preview, when the vault locks and after a fresh app launch.

Sharing a document sends a readable copy to the app or person you choose. That copy is outside the vault's protection.

If you only need to stop edits to a PDF, locking a PDF in Files is the built-in option.

Limits

  • Importing copies a file. The original stays in Files until you delete it.
  • Notes are plain text. There's no rich formatting or attachments inside a note.
