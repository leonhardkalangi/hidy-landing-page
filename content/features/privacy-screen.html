Why this matters

Most privacy slips happen in seconds: you switch to Messages, hand the phone over, and the last app you used is one swipe away. A vault that stays open after you leave is only as private as the next person to pick up the phone.

What Hidy does

  • Locks on background. Going to the Home Screen, switching apps or locking the iPhone ends the vault session.
  • Covers the app switcher. The preview shows a cover instead of your files.
  • Covers open viewers. A separate privacy window covers photo, video and document viewers while Hidy is inactive.
  • Cleans up. Temporary decrypted files used for viewing or sharing are cleared when the vault locks.

Where it stops

  • An open vault is visible. If you hand over the phone with a vault open on screen, the person sees it.
  • Screen capture is possible while open. Hidy covers its content when inactive, not while you're actively using it.
  • Not a defence against an unlocked, compromised device. The security page spells out the boundaries.

Habits that help

  1. Lock Hidy before you hand someone the phone.
  2. To show one photo, open it in a vault that holds nothing else sensitive. See show one photo without letting someone swipe.
  3. Keep the most private files behind a pattern you never draw in front of anyone.
