Before you start

  • Choose which vault the photos belong in. Separate patterns make separate vaults.
  • Check the import quality in Hidy's Settings. Optimized (default) saves space and removes location data. Original keeps files exactly as they are. See location removal.

Step by step

  1. Open the vault. Draw its pattern in Hidy Vault.
  2. Import. Tap add, choose Photos, and select the photos and videos. Hidy encrypts copies, names and thumbnails.
  3. Delete the originals. Hidy offers to delete them from your library. iOS asks you to confirm.
  4. Empty Recently Deleted. In Photos, go to Collections > Utilities > Recently Deleted, unlock it, tap Select, and delete the items.
  5. Check. Open the vault and view a few photos and videos before you rely on it.

Other copies to think about

  • Shared albums keep their own copies for everyone in them.
  • Messages and chat apps keep photos you sent or received.
  • Computer backups made before you moved the photos still contain them.

Then protect the vault

Create the vault's recovery phrase and turn on encrypted iCloud backup. Your photos now exist in one place; make sure you can't lose it.
