Is there a Face ID bypass for the Hidden album?

No. Apple does not document any way to skip Face ID on the Hidden album. Apple's documented unlock methods are Face ID, Touch ID or the iPhone passcode. On iOS 16 and later, the Hidden album is locked by default.

That means the question "how do I bypass Face ID" usually has a simpler answer: use the passcode. It is not a bypass. It is one of the documented ways in.

How the Hidden album actually unlocks

  1. Open Photos.
  2. Go to Collections and scroll to Hidden under Utilities.
  3. Tap View Album.
  4. Authenticate with Face ID, Touch ID or the passcode.

Apple documents the same three methods for Recently Deleted. There is no separate Hidden album password.

What you can control

Hide the album from Photos

Go to Settings > Apps > Photos and turn off Show Hidden Album. The album no longer appears in Photos, and the photos stay hidden. This changes visibility, not authentication.

Hide a photo in the first place

In Photos, touch and hold the photo or video, tap Hide, then confirm. To hide several at once, tap Select, choose them, then More > Hide. Hidden items leave the Library, other albums and the Photos widget.

Why the passcode is the real weak point

Face ID is not the only key. Apple documents the passcode as an unlock method for the Hidden album. If someone knows your passcode, the Face ID lock is not the barrier you might think.

This matters in the situations people actually worry about: a partner who knows the passcode, a family member who watched you type it, or a phone that is handed over unlocked. Our guide to a partner who knows your iPhone passcode goes through the options.

What the Hidden album does not protect against

  • A known passcode. Apple documents the passcode as an unlock method.
  • iCloud Photos. With iCloud Photos on, hidden on one device means hidden on the others. The photos are still in your iCloud Photos library.
  • Someone holding your unlocked phone. The album is a few taps away once the phone is open.

None of this makes the Hidden album useless. It is a quick way to keep photos out of the main grid. It is not a private safe.

Limits you should know

  • Hidy cannot reset a forgotten pattern. A 12-word recovery phrase per vault is the way back in.
  • Do not uninstall Hidy to troubleshoot. Local vaults and the Keychain secret can be lost.
  • Someone examining the app's storage can see approximate file sizes and the number of vault folders, not contents or names.
  • There has been no independent cryptographic audit of Hidy yet.
  • Sharing a file out of Hidy gives the recipient a readable copy.
