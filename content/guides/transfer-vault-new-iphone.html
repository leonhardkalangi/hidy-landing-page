On the old iPhone

  1. Open each vault and go to Settings.
  2. Create a recovery phrase if it doesn't have one, and write it down.
  3. Turn on iCloud backup, or export a backup file to Files, iCloud Drive, a Mac or AirDrop it to the new phone.
  4. Wait until the backup completes.

On the new iPhone

  1. Install Hidy Vault.
  2. On the lock screen, open the options and choose Restore from iCloud or Restore from a backup file.
  3. Pick the backup, enter the vault's 12 words, and choose a pattern.
  4. Repeat for each vault.

Before you erase the old iPhone

  • Open every vault on the new iPhone and view a few files.
  • Keep the old iPhone and the backups until you've checked.
  • Then follow before you sell your iPhone.
