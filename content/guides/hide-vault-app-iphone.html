Option 1: Hide and lock it (iOS 18 and later)

  1. On the Home Screen, touch and hold the app.
  2. Tap Require Face ID (or Touch ID or Passcode).
  3. Tap Hide and Require Face ID, authenticate, then tap Hide App.

Apple says a hidden app disappears from the Home Screen, App Library and search results, and moves to a Hidden folder at the bottom of App Library that needs Face ID to open. Only apps you downloaded can be hidden.

To open it, swipe past your last Home Screen page to App Library, tap Hidden at the bottom, and authenticate.

Option 2: Remove it from the Home Screen

Touch and hold the icon, tap Remove App, then Remove from Home Screen. The app stays installed and findable in App Library and search. Quick, but not really hidden.

Option 3: A disguised icon (Hidy Vault Pro)

In Hidy's Settings > App icon, choose Calculator, Notes, Weather or Tasks. The name under the icon stays Hidy Vault. See disguised app icons.

What none of these hide

  • App Store purchase history.
  • Screen Time usage reports.
  • The app's entry in Settings.

Hiding the app is camouflage. What protects the files is the vault's encryption: in Hidy Vault, nothing opens without a vault's pattern.
