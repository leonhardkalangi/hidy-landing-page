Hidy shared vault (Pro)An encrypted copy in their Hidy VaultStop future syncing; existing copies stay

Before you send anything

  1. Remove location. In the share sheet, tap Options and turn off Location. Or strip everything with the metadata remover.
  2. Check what's in the frame. Reflections, documents, addresses and faces you didn't mean to include.
  3. Think about their phone. Who else uses it? Is their library synced to a family iPad?

Share an encrypted vault instead

For a set of photos you keep adding to, a shared vault avoids the camera roll on both sides. Create a separate vault with only what you want to share, then create an invite in its Settings: 12 words that expire after 48 hours. The other person joins from their Hidy lock screen. See secure sharing.

Hidy Vault sharing is part of Pro.
