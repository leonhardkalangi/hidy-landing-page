iPhone photo recovery after delete

Updated 15 November 2026 · By Hidy

Open Photos, scroll to Utilities, tap Recently Deleted, unlock it, then tap the photo and Recover. Deleted photos stay there for 30 days. After that they are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

Quick answer
  • Photos > Utilities > Recently Deleted, unlock with Face ID or Touch ID.
  • Tap the photo, tap Recover, then confirm. It returns to your library.
  • After 30 days the item is permanently deleted. No recovery after that.

Where do deleted iPhone photos go?

They go to Recently Deleted, an album inside Photos. Apple keeps them there for 30 days. During that window you can put them back. After 30 days they are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

Recently Deleted is locked. Apple says it opens with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. You can change that lock in Settings > Apps > Photos > Use Face ID.

How do I recover one deleted photo?

  1. Open Photos and swipe up to see Utilities.
  2. Tap Recently Deleted.
  3. Tap View Album, then unlock with Face ID or Touch ID.
  4. Tap the photo or video.
  5. Tap Recover, then confirm.

The item returns to your Photos library.

How do I recover several photos at once?

  1. Open Recently Deleted and unlock it.
  2. Tap Select.
  3. Tap each photo or video you want back.
  4. Tap the More button, then tap Recover.
  5. Confirm.

Do this before the 30 days run out. There is no bulk recovery after the item leaves Recently Deleted.

What is the Recovered album in iOS 18?

In iOS 18, Photos may show a Recovered album under Utilities. Apple says it appears when the iPhone finds photos or videos on the device that are not currently in your library. It is not always shown.

To use it: open Photos, tap Collections, scroll down, then tap Recovered under Utilities. Unlock it with Face ID, Touch ID or Optic ID. Select an item, then tap Permanently Delete or Restore to Library.

Be careful here. Deleting an item from Recovered bypasses Recently Deleted, happens immediately, and cannot be undone. If you are unsure, restore it to the library first and decide later.

Why is my deleted photo not in Recently Deleted?

Check these in order:

  • 30 days passed. Apple permanently deletes items after 30 days.
  • iCloud storage is over its limit. Apple says deleted photos and videos are then removed immediately and do not go to Recently Deleted.
  • You deleted it from the Recovered album. That skips Recently Deleted entirely.

If none of these apply, the photo is likely gone. Apple does not offer a second recovery window.

What changes with iCloud Photos on?

With iCloud Photos, your library is the same on every device signed in with your Apple Account. Apple says deleting a photo or video on one device also removes it from iCloud and from every device using iCloud Photos. Recently Deleted recovery applies across those devices.

That cuts both ways. A delete on your iPad removes the photo from your iPhone too. A recovery on your iPhone brings it back on the iPad.

How do I avoid needing recovery at all?

  • Keep a copy outside Photos. Apple recommends keeping your own backup copies of your library. Export originals to Files or a computer before big cleanups.
  • Watch iCloud storage. A full account removes the 30-day safety net for new deletes.
  • Move, do not delete. If you want photos off the camera roll but not gone, import them somewhere you control first, then delete.
  • Check before you confirm. The delete confirmation is the last easy moment.

Limits you should know

  • There is no recovery after 30 days. Apple states this plainly.
  • If iCloud storage is full, a delete can skip Recently Deleted entirely.
  • Deleting from the Recovered album is immediate and cannot be undone.
  • Hidy cannot recover photos deleted from your Photos library. It only protects what you put inside it.
  • Hidy has no Face ID unlock and no account recovery. A forgotten pattern needs that vault's 12-word recovery phrase.

If the photo is already gone, the honest answer is that no app can bring it back. The useful work is preventing the next loss: a copy you control, and a locked place for the photos you never want in the camera roll.

Frequently asked questions

How long do deleted photos stay on my iPhone?

Apple keeps them in Recently Deleted for 30 days. After 30 days they are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

I deleted a photo and it is not in Recently Deleted. Why?

Two common reasons: the 30 days passed, or your iCloud storage is over its limit. Apple says deleted photos and videos are then removed immediately and do not go to Recently Deleted.

Does recovering a photo on one device bring it back on my other devices?

With iCloud Photos on, deleting removes the photo from every device using iCloud Photos, and recovery applies across those devices.

What is the Recovered album in Photos?

In iOS 18, Photos may show a Recovered album under Utilities when the iPhone finds photos or videos on the device that are not in your library. It is not always shown.

Can I undo a delete from the Recovered album?

No. Deleting an item from Recovered skips Recently Deleted, happens immediately, and cannot be undone.
