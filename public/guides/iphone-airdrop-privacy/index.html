iPhone AirDrop privacy: who can send you files

Updated 21 November 2026 · By Hidy

Open Settings > General > AirDrop and choose Receiving Off, Contacts Only, or Everyone for 10 Minutes. Everyone for 10 Minutes resets by itself, so you are not left open indefinitely.

Quick answer
  • Settings > General > AirDrop: Receiving Off, Contacts Only, or Everyone for 10 Minutes.
  • Everyone for 10 Minutes resets to Contacts Only (signed in) or Receiving Off (signed out).
  • AirDrop needs Bluetooth and Wi-Fi on, both devices unlocked, and about 30 feet (10 meters) of distance.

What can strangers send me over AirDrop?

With Everyone for 10 Minutes, a nearby device can offer you a file for ten minutes. You still choose to accept or decline each transfer. Apple says AirDrop transfers are encrypted, and the recipient can accept or decline each one.

The risk is not a silent file landing on your iPhone. The risk is the offer itself: a stranger can see that your device is reachable, and can send you a preview to accept. If you would rather not be reachable at all, choose Receiving Off.

Which AirDrop setting should I use?

SettingWho can send to youWhen it resets
Receiving OffNobodyStays off until you change it
Contacts OnlyPeople in your Contacts, matched by Apple Account email or phone numberStays on until you change it
Everyone for 10 MinutesAny nearby deviceAfter 10 minutes: Contacts Only if signed in, Receiving Off if not

For everyday use, Contacts Only is the practical middle. Turn on Everyone for 10 Minutes only when you are handing a file to someone who is not in your Contacts, then let it expire.

How do I change the setting from Control Center?

  1. Open Control Center.
  2. Touch and hold the top-left group of controls.
  3. Tap AirDrop.
  4. Choose Contacts Only or Everyone for 10 Minutes.

This is faster than going through Settings, and it is the same switch. The Settings path is Settings > General > AirDrop.

What does Contacts Only actually check?

Apple says the sender's Apple Account email address or phone number must be in your Contacts card. It is not a check of who the person is in real life. If a stranger has your email address saved in their own contacts, that does not matter; the check is on your side.

If a contact's Apple Account details are not saved on your iPhone, they may not appear as a Contacts Only sender. In that case, use Everyone for 10 Minutes briefly, or add the details to their contact card first.

Can an AirDrop transfer continue after I leave?

Yes, if Use Cellular Data is on. Apple says an AirDrop transfer can continue over the internet after you leave Bluetooth or Wi-Fi range. That is useful for a large video, but it means the transfer is no longer limited to nearby devices.

To stop it, go to Settings > General > AirDrop and turn off Use Cellular Data. Then a transfer that leaves range simply stops.

What are the requirements for AirDrop to work?

  • Wi-Fi and Bluetooth turned on.
  • Both devices awake and unlocked.
  • Devices within about 30 feet (10 meters).
  • Personal Hotspot turned off while using AirDrop.

Because both devices must be unlocked, a locked iPhone in your bag is not receiving AirDrop offers. That is one reason to keep the lock screen short and the passcode on.

How do I start a share by bringing devices together?

Go to Settings > General > AirDrop > Start Sharing By and set it to Bringing Devices Together. Then hold your iPhone near another iPhone to start a share. This is a convenience setting, not a privacy one, but it is worth knowing if you share files often.

Can I turn AirDrop off completely?

Yes. Choose Receiving Off in Settings > General > AirDrop. Apple also says Screen Time > Content & Privacy Restrictions can turn AirDrop off. That is useful on a child's iPhone or a work-managed device.

Receiving Off stops your iPhone from receiving AirDrop items. It does not stop you from sending files to someone else.

What AirDrop settings do not protect

AirDrop settings control who can offer you a file. They do not control what happens to a file after you accept it. An accepted photo lands in Photos, where it sits in your library like any other photo.

Frequently asked questions

Does AirDrop work if my iPhone is locked?

No. Apple says both devices must be awake and unlocked, with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on, and within about 30 feet (10 meters).

What happens after the 10 minutes of Everyone?

It changes to Contacts Only if you are signed in to an Apple Account, or to Receiving Off if you are not.

Can an AirDrop transfer continue after I walk away?

Yes, if Use Cellular Data is on. Apple says the transfer can continue over the internet after you leave Bluetooth or Wi-Fi range. Turn it off in Settings > General > AirDrop.

Are AirDrop transfers encrypted?

Apple says AirDrop transfers are encrypted, and the recipient can accept or decline each one.

Can I turn AirDrop off completely?

Yes. Choose Receiving Off, or use Screen Time > Content & Privacy Restrictions to turn AirDrop off.
