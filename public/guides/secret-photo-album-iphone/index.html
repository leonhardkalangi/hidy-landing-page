Secret photo album on iPhone: what iOS hides and what it doesn't

Updated 27 November 2026 · By Hidy

The closest thing to a secret photo album on iPhone is the Hidden album in Photos. It is locked by default, and you can hide the album itself in Settings. It is not a separate encrypted space, though: hidden photos still live in your photo library and follow iCloud Photos.

Quick answer
  • Hide a photo: touch and hold it in Photos > Hide.
  • Open it: Collections > Utilities > Hidden > View Album, then authenticate.
  • Hide the album: Settings > Apps > Photos > turn off Show Hidden Album.

What is the closest thing to a secret photo album on iPhone?

It is the Hidden album in Photos. Apple moved it out of your main library: hidden photos no longer appear in the Library, in other albums, or in the Photos widget. On iOS 16 and later the album is locked by default and opens with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.

That is a real improvement over the old behavior. It is still not a separate, encrypted album. The photos remain part of your photo library, and the lock is the same one that opens your iPhone.

How do I hide a photo or video?

  1. Open Photos and find the photo or video.
  2. Touch and hold it, then tap Hide.
  3. Confirm when iOS asks.

For several items at once, tap Select, choose the photos, tap More (the three dots), then Hide. Apple documents both routes on its Hide photos and videos page.

How do I open the Hidden album?

  1. Open Photos and tap Collections.
  2. Scroll to Utilities and tap Hidden.
  3. Tap View Album and authenticate with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.

To bring a photo back to the library, touch and hold it inside the Hidden album and tap Unhide.

Can I hide the Hidden album itself?

Yes. Go to Settings > Apps > Photos and turn off Show Hidden Album. The album stops appearing in Photos. Your hidden photos stay where they are, and the album comes back when you turn the setting on again.

This is useful if you do not want the album listed at all. It does not add protection. Anyone who knows the setting can switch it back on, and the album still opens with the device passcode.

What does the Hidden album not protect?

  • Your passcode. The Hidden album unlocks with Face ID, Touch ID or the iPhone passcode. Someone who knows your passcode can open it.
  • iCloud Photos. With iCloud Photos on, Apple says hiding a photo hides it on every device signed in with the same Apple Account that uses iCloud Photos. The photo is still in your iCloud Photos library.

None of this makes the Hidden album useless. It keeps private photos out of casual swiping. It is not built to survive someone who knows your passcode and looks for it.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Hidden album a real secret photo album?

No. It is a locked view of photos that still belong to your library. Anyone who can open the album with your passcode can see everything in it.

Do hidden photos sync to iCloud?

With iCloud Photos on, Apple says hiding a photo hides it on every device signed in with the same Apple Account that uses iCloud Photos. The photo is still part of your iCloud Photos library.

Can I hide the Hidden album completely?

Yes. Turn off Show Hidden Album in Settings > Apps > Photos. The album disappears from Photos, and the photos stay hidden until you turn it back on.

What happens to hidden photos if I delete the Photos app?

Apple says deleting an app removes the app and its data from the iPhone. Our guide on deleting a vault app covers what that means for a vault.
