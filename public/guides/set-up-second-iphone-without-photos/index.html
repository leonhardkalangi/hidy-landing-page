How to set up a second iPhone without photos

Updated 13 November 2026 · By Hidy

Set up the new iPhone as a fresh device and skip the data transfer or backup restore. Apple's setup screens do not offer a way to copy everything except Photos, so the clean route is the only reliable one.

Quick answer
  • Quick Start: when asked to transfer data, choose the option to set up without transferring.
  • Apps & Data: do not tap Restore from iCloud Backup or Restore from Mac or PC.
  • After setup: decide whether iCloud Photos should sync on the second iPhone.

Why a fresh setup is the only clean way

Apple's setup screens are built around copying a device, not trimming one. Quick Start can copy settings, preferences and iCloud Keychain, and a wireless transfer can move all your data. A backup restore brings back personal data and purchased content.

None of those paths has a checkbox that says "skip Photos." Apple's setup instructions do not document a setting that transfers iCloud data while leaving the photo library out. So if you want a second iPhone without your photos, you set it up as a new device and skip the transfer and the restore.

That is the honest answer. It costs you some convenience, and this guide covers what you give up.

Before you start: two things to decide

  • Same Apple Account or a different one? The account decides what can sync later. iCloud Photos follows the account, not the device.
  • What does the second iPhone need? If it only needs calls, messages and a few apps, a fresh setup is easy. If it needs years of app data, plan for manual work.

If the second iPhone is for a child or a shared family device, read a shared Apple Account or family iPad first. The account choice matters more than the setup steps.

Step by step: set up the second iPhone without photos

  1. Turn on the new iPhone and place it near your current iPhone if you want Quick Start. Otherwise tap Set Up Without Another Device.
  2. Follow the onscreen instructions: language, region, Wi-Fi. Setup needs a Wi-Fi or cellular connection to activate and continue.
  3. When Quick Start offers to transfer data, choose the option to set up without transferring.
  4. At the Apps & Data screen, do not tap Restore from iCloud Backup.
  5. Do not tap Restore from Mac or PC either. A restore transfers that backup's data to the iPhone.
  6. Continue as a new iPhone and sign in with your Apple Account when asked.
  7. Open Settings and check whether iCloud Photos is syncing on this device. If you want the library kept off it, see the next section.

Quick Start needs the current iPhone on iOS 11 or later. A full wireless data transfer needs both devices on iOS 12.4 or later. You are skipping that transfer, so the second number matters less here.

The catch: iCloud Photos can still download your library

This is the part most guides skip. A fresh setup keeps the old backup off the device, but it does not change what is already in your iCloud account. If iCloud Photos is on for your Apple Account, photos and videos stored there can download to the second iPhone after you sign in.

So the setup is only half the job. The other half is deciding what iCloud Photos should do on this device. Our guide on stopping iCloud Photos from syncing to other devices walks through the account-level choices.

If you are unsure what is already stored, read are hidden photos saved to iCloud? before you sign in on the new phone.

If the second iPhone is already set up

You can start over. Go to Settings > General > Transfer or Reset iPhone > Erase All Content and Settings. Erasing removes all data and returns the iPhone to the setup screen.

Back up anything on that device you want to keep first. Apple's before you sell or trade in page covers the same erase path and the sign-out steps around it. Our wipe guide has the checklist.

Limits to know

  • There is no Apple setting that transfers everything except Photos. You choose between a full copy and a fresh start.
  • A fresh setup does not remove photos from iCloud. It only keeps the old backup off the new device.
  • iCloud Photos behavior depends on the Apple Account, so a second device signed into the same account can still receive the library.
  • App data that lived only on the old iPhone is not recoverable through setup.
  • Hidy cannot reset a forgotten pattern. Set up the recovery phrase for any vault you rely on.

Plan the account and the iCloud Photos choice before you power on the new iPhone. Those two decisions do more than any setup screen.

Frequently asked questions

Can I transfer everything except Photos during setup?

No. Apple's setup instructions do not document a setting that copies iCloud data while leaving Photos out. You either transfer or restore the data, or you start fresh.

Will my photos appear on the second iPhone anyway?

They can, if you sign in with the same Apple Account and iCloud Photos is on for that account. Photos already in iCloud Photos can download to the new device.

What do I lose with a fresh setup?

App data that lived only on the old iPhone, plus settings you had customized. Apps can be downloaded again, but their local data may not come back.

Can I fix this if the second iPhone is already set up?

Yes. Settings > General > Transfer or Reset iPhone > Erase All Content and Settings returns it to the setup screen. Back up anything you want to keep first.

Does the second iPhone need a connection to finish setup?

Yes. Setup needs Wi-Fi or cellular to activate and continue.
