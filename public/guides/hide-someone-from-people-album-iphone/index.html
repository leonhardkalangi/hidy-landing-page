How to hide someone from the People album on iPhone

Updated 18 November 2026 · By Hidy

You cannot remove a person from the People & Pets album, but you can stop their face appearing in Memories, Featured Photos and the Photos widget. Open the person in People & Pets, tap the … button, and choose Feature This Person Less or Never Feature This Person.

Quick answer
  • People & Pets: Photos > Collections > People & Pets > tap the person > … > Feature This Person Less.
  • Choose Never Feature This Person to stop them appearing in Memories, Featured Photos and the widget.
  • Their photos stay in your library and in People & Pets. This is not hiding.

What does "hide someone from the People album" actually mean on iPhone?

Photos groups faces it recognizes into People & Pets. Apple gives you a way to feature a person less often, or never. It does not give you a way to remove a person from that album.

So the honest answer is: you can stop someone's face showing up in Memories, Featured Photos and the Photos widget. You cannot make their photos disappear from the People & Pets album itself.

That difference matters. If your goal is to stop a person appearing on your lock screen or in a slideshow, the steps below work. If your goal is to keep their photos away from someone holding your phone, you need a different tool.

How do I stop one person appearing in Memories and Featured Photos?

Do this from the person's own page. It is the fastest route and it applies to every photo of them.

  1. Open Photos.
  2. Tap Collections, then People & Pets.
  3. Tap the person you want to feature less.
  4. Tap the … button.
  5. Choose Feature This Person Less or Never Feature This Person.
  6. Tap Confirm.

Apple says these options reduce or prevent the person from appearing in Memories, Featured Photos and the Photos widget. "Never Feature This Person" is the stronger of the two.

Nothing is deleted. Their photos stay in your library, in your albums, and in People & Pets.

Can I do it from a single photo instead?

Yes. This is useful when one photo keeps resurfacing and you want to deal with it directly.

  1. Open the photo in Photos.
  2. Tap the … button.
  3. Choose Feature This Person Less.
  4. Pick Feature This Person Less or Never Feature This Person.
  5. Tap Confirm.

If more than one person is in the photo, select the right face first, then tap Next. Otherwise you may reduce the wrong person by mistake.

What if I want to turn off all featured content?

You can switch off the whole feature instead of one face. Go to Settings > Apps > Photos and turn off Show Featured Content.

Apple says this turns off Memories and Featured Photos in the Photos app and in the Photos widget. It is a blunt tool: no one gets featured, including people you like seeing.

Use it if you want a quiet photo library with no automatic resurfacing at all. Use the per-person option if you only want one face gone.

Can I undo it later?

Yes. You can reset all of it: in Photos, open Collections, tap your account icon in the top right, and choose Reset People & Pets Suggestions.

Resetting clears the suggestions Photos has built up. It does not delete photos.

What these settings do not do

This is the part most guides skip. Apple's documented controls affect Memories, Featured Photos and the Photos widget. They do not hide or remove the person's images from your Photos library or from the People & Pets album.

So if someone picks up your unlocked iPhone and opens Photos, they can still find that person's pictures. The face is just less likely to be pushed at you automatically.

Other things these settings do not change:

  • The person's photos in your main library.
  • Their entry in People & Pets.
  • Anything already shared through iCloud Shared Photo Library.

Limits worth knowing

  • People & Pets recognition is automatic. It can group faces you did not expect, and it can miss people.
  • "Feature This Person Less" is a reduction, not a guarantee. Apple's wording is that it reduces appearances.
  • These settings are about featured content. They are not a privacy lock.

Frequently asked questions

Does this delete the person's photos?

No. Their photos stay in your library and in the People & Pets album. The setting only affects where their face is featured.

Can I remove someone from People & Pets entirely?

Apple's controls reduce or stop featuring a person. They do not remove the person from the People & Pets album.

Does it work for pets too?

People & Pets covers both people and pets. Apple documents the Feature This Person Less options for people.

Can I undo it?

Yes. You can reset all suggestions: in Photos, open Collections, tap your account icon in the top right, and choose Reset People & Pets Suggestions.
