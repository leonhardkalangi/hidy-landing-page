Hidy Vault vs calculator vault apps

Updated 22 October 2026 · By Hidy

Calculator vault apps look like a working calculator and open a photo vault when you type a code. The disguise can be convincing. What protects your photos, though, is what happens behind it, and that varies from app to app.

Quick answer
  • A calculator disguise hides that a vault exists. It doesn't by itself protect the files.
  • Check each app's listing for how files are encrypted, where backups go, and whether there are ads or an account.
  • Hidy only borrows the look of a calculator icon (Pro). The protection is the pattern-derived encryption.

Disguise and encryption are different jobs

A disguise answers "is there a vault on this phone?". Encryption answers "if someone finds it, can they read it?". A calculator vault is very good at the first question. Whether it answers the second depends entirely on the app.

Five checks before you trust any vault app

  1. Encryption on the device. Does the listing say files are encrypted on the phone, not only in the cloud?
  2. Who holds the key. If the company can reset your code by email, it can also get to the data.
  3. Backups. Are backups encrypted with something only you know? What happens if you lose the phone?
  4. Ads and trackers. Check the App Privacy section on the App Store page.
  5. What it admits. A trustworthy app tells you its limits. Be wary of "100% unhackable".

Frequently asked questions

Are calculator vault apps safe?

Some are and some aren't. The calculator is a disguise; safety depends on whether files are encrypted on the device, whether backups are encrypted with a key only you hold, and how the company handles your data. Read the App Store listing and privacy label.

Can someone tell a calculator vault apart from the real Calculator?

Often, yes. The App Library, Settings, Screen Time and the App Store's purchase history all show the real app name. A disguise works against a glance, not a search.

Does Hidy have a fake calculator?

No. With Pro you can use a Calculator-style icon, but Hidy opens to its pattern grid. Its name under the icon stays Hidy Vault.
