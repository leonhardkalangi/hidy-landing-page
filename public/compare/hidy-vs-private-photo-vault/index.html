Hidy Vault vs Private Photo Vault

Updated 22 October 2026 · By Hidy

Private Photo Vault (Pic Safe) is a feature-rich vault that has been on the App Store since 2011: PIN or pattern lock, break-in reports, a decoy password and a private browser. Hidy Vault does fewer things, with vault keys derived from your pattern and free encrypted backup.

Quick answer
  • Private Photo Vault, per its listing: PIN or pattern lock, password-protected albums, break-in report with photo and GPS, decoy password, private browser, encrypted Cloud Vault (Premium).
  • Hidy Vault: pattern-derived encryption on the device, documents and notes, free encrypted iCloud backup, decoy vault and sharing (Pro).
  • Want break-in photos and a private browser? Private Photo Vault has them; Hidy doesn't.

Where Private Photo Vault is stronger

  • More features: break-in reports, a private browser, face-down lock and wireless transfer.
  • iPad support and a long track record since 2011.
Want photos your iPhone passcode can't open?

Hidy Vault keeps photos, videos and documents in encrypted vaults that open only with a pattern you draw. No account, no Hidy server. Free for 3 vaults.

Where Hidy is different

  • Encryption described for the device itself. Hidy encrypts every file, name and thumbnail on the iPhone with a key derived from the pattern. See pattern encryption.
  • Free backup. Encrypted iCloud backup and backup files are in the free plan.
  • Deniable vaults. With one vault per pattern and no wrong-pattern state, Hidy doesn't reveal how many vaults exist.
  • Documents and notes alongside photos.

How to decide

If you want to know when someone tried to get in, or you need iPad, Private Photo Vault covers that. If you want fewer features and a vault whose key you alone hold, try Hidy Vault. It's free for 3 vaults.

Frequently asked questions

Does Hidy take a photo of someone who enters the wrong code?

No. Hidy has no wrong-pattern state at all: any valid pattern opens a vault, and an unused one opens an empty space. That's what keeps other vaults deniable.

Is Private Photo Vault's backup free?

Its listing says the free version does not back up files to Cloud Vault. Hidy's encrypted iCloud backup is included in the free plan.

Does Private Photo Vault work on iPad?

Its listing describes the app for iPhone and iPad. Hidy Vault is built for iPhone.
